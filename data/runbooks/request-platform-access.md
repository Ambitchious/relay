# Requesting access to a platform service

**Who this is for.** Engineers who need access to Spark, Airflow, Trino, Druid
or the shared notebooks environment.

## Steps

1. Open an access request in the platform portal under *Access > New Request*.
2. Pick the service and the environment. Production access needs your manager
   named as approver; staging does not.
3. State the business reason in one line. Requests without one are bounced.
4. For Airflow, specify whether you need read-only UI access or the ability to
   trigger and clear tasks — these are different roles.

## Timing

Standard requests are processed within one business day. Production access to
Trino additionally needs data-governance sign-off and takes two to three days.

## Common rejections

- Requesting production when staging would do.
- Asking for a role rather than naming what you need to do with it.
